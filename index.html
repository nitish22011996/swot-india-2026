Introduction

Why This Course

This program brings together researchers and students working on the Surface Water and Ocean Topography (SWOT) mission, launched in December 2022, with a focus on SWOT related studies over Indian basins and new collaborations in river and lake science.

  • 01Overview of SWOT hydrology products, quality control procedures, and recommendations for users.
  • 02Recent scientific advances from the SWOT Science Team across hydrology related topics.
  • 03New synergies and collaborations for SWOT based research over India.

Course Outline

Fundamentals of SWOT hydrology products, quality control steps, and an overview of SWOT discharge algorithms. Includes demonstrations and practical sessions in data handling, plus the latest applications in hydrological modelling.
